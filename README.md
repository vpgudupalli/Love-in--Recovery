# Recovery in Love

Recovery in Love is an adults-only dating platform designed for people who want relationships compatible with their recovery, lifestyle, and relationship goals.

## Core product areas
- Account creation and onboarding
- Public dating profile and photos
- Recovery preferences and sobriety information
- Optional mental-health disclosure with privacy controls
- Attachment and Enneagram assessment provider integrations
- Recovery compatibility questionnaire
- Compatibility-based discovery
- Like / pass / mutual match flow
- Messaging
- Block / unmatch / report
- Relationship verification and private safety concerns
- Trust & Safety admin dashboard
- Account privacy and deletion

## Product principles
- Recovery and mental-health information is sensitive and user-controlled.
- Assessments support self-awareness and matching; they are not diagnoses.
- Relationship concerns and evidence are reviewed privately, not posted publicly.
- Compatibility explanations should be based only on structured data users agreed to use.
- Adults 18+ only.

## Stack
